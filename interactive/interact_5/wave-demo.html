Interactive Ripple Explorer

Move or touch the image to move the ripple center. The image and graph visualize the same three sine waves, distance falloff, and texture offset used by this lesson's fragment shader. The demo starts at frequency 16 to make the rings easier to see; set it to 8 to match the shader's default.

Enhanced view adds a false-color highlight based on the signed wave value. Choose “Final shader color” to see the unmodified shader blend. The dashed radius and yellow center marker are guides.

Wave profile by distance

The graph plots the waves and falloff against distance from the center.

The upper graph shows each sine wave and their sum. The lower graph shows the smooth falloff. The vertical marker is the current radius.

This dependency-free Canvas illustration evaluates the fragment shader's equations with a generated test pattern; it does not compile or execute GLSL. The displayed coordinates use U right and V down for convenient screen interaction. A real renderer must map mouse coordinates into the same UV convention as its geometry.
